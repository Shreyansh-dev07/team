What Can You Report?

Select any public infrastructure issue in your neighborhood. SudhaarSetu automatically routes your report to the designated Kanpur government division.

Road & Infrastructure

Routes to: Road / Public Works Department

Report road cracks, hazardous potholes, damaged asphalt, sinking pavements, or broken pedestrian footpaths across Kanpur.

Simple 5-Step Process

How SudhaarSetu Works for Citizens

Transparent grievance redressal from submission to ground inspection and verified resolution.

1

1. Report

Select the issue category and upload a clear photo of the problem.

2

2. Locate

Use GPS, interactive Kanpur map, or select your Kanpur ward & landmark.

3

3. Forward

System instantly routes the grievance to the concerned Kanpur municipal department.

4

4. Track

Receive a unique Complaint ID (e.g. SS-KNP-2026-001245) to monitor live status.

5

5. Resolve

Field engineering squads resolve the issue and upload photographic evidence.
